Blinkit Grocery Sales & Customer Insights

Personal Project · Python, Pandas, NumPy, Power BI
  • Question: which categories, outlet formats and city tiers drive revenue, and does shelf visibility actually lift sales?
  • Data quality: across 8,523 records, 1,559 products and 10 outlets, I collapsed 5 inconsistent fat-content labels into 2, imputed 1,463 missing item weights, and treated 526 impossible zero-visibility values as missing.
  • Findings: Low Fat products drive 64.6% of revenue; Supermarket Type1 outlets generate about 66%; Tier 3 cities (472.1K) outsell Tier 1 (336.4K); shelf visibility shows essentially no correlation with sales (−0.005).
  • Recommendation: prioritise Medium-size Supermarket Type1 outlets in Tier 3 cities, and look at pricing and assortment rather than visibility to lift sales.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with slicers and KPI views for total sales, average sales, item count and ratings.
